# 1.2 Domain Interaction Matrix

Status: Canonical

This matrix defines the permitted enterprise interactions between ACOS bounded contexts. It complements `config/domains/registry.yaml`; the registry remains the machine-readable source of truth.

## Interaction rules

1. A domain may mutate only data it owns.
2. Cross-domain state changes use explicit commands or versioned events.
3. Platform domains coordinate or transport; they do not acquire business ownership.
4. Governance decisions precede material side effects and fail closed where policy requires.
5. External providers are reached only through Provider Integration anti-corruption layers.
6. Every material interaction carries correlation, causation, identity and audit metadata.

## Prohibited interactions

- Direct database writes across bounded contexts.
- Provider SDK calls from commerce, intelligence or governance domains.
- Runtime-owned copies becoming authoritative business records.
- Marketing changing brand rules or budget decisions.
- AI-generated product acceptance, authority expansion or constitutional change.
- Unversioned public events.

## Change control

A new interaction requires:

- named producer and consumer;
- ownership and authority analysis;
- command, service or event contract;
- failure, retry and idempotency semantics;
- governance and audit requirements;
- registry and diagram updates;
- CI validation before release.
