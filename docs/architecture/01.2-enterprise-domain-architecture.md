# 1.2 Enterprise Domain Architecture

**Status:** Draft canonical foundation  
**Architecture layer:** Enterprise Architecture  
**Depends on:** ACOS Constitution, Chapter 1.0 Enterprise Context, Chapter 1.1 Enterprise Capability Architecture

## Purpose

This chapter translates the enterprise capability model into explicit domain boundaries. It defines which bounded context owns which business concepts, decisions, data and events, and how domains may interact without leaking implementation detail or surrendering authority.

The domain architecture is designed to preserve four properties:

1. **Clear ownership** — every material concept, decision and record has one authoritative domain owner.
2. **Controlled interaction** — domains collaborate only through explicit contracts, events or governed application services.
3. **Replaceability** — external providers and technical implementations may change without changing enterprise semantics.
4. **Governance by design** — constitutional, policy, risk, budget, identity and audit controls remain outside commerce implementation detail and cannot be bypassed.

## Domain model

ACOS is organized into four enterprise domain groups.

| Domain group | Strategic responsibility | Authority profile |
|---|---|---|
| Commerce | Operate the commercial lifecycle from product discovery through order, fulfillment and outcome analysis | Executes only within delegated authority and governance constraints |
| Intelligence | Convert evidence into recommendations, plans and coordinated action proposals | May analyze, recommend and coordinate; may not expand its own authority |
| Governance | Decide whether actions are constitutionally and operationally permitted | Fail-closed authority over policy, approval, risk, budget, security and audit controls |
| Platform | Provide trusted identity, durable execution, events, integrations, knowledge, observability and configuration | Enables execution but does not own commerce policy or business outcomes |

## Bounded contexts

### Commerce domain group

#### Product

**Purpose:** Own product candidates, qualification evidence, product lifecycle and product publishing decisions after required human acceptance.

**Owns:**

- product candidate
- product qualification record
- product lifecycle state
- product specification
- product publication state

**Does not own:** supplier truth, brand identity, campaign strategy, budget authority or product acceptance approval.

#### Supplier

**Purpose:** Discover, assess and maintain the authoritative enterprise view of supplier suitability and supplier relationships.

**Owns:**

- supplier candidate
- supplier profile
- supplier qualification record
- supplier terms snapshot
- supplier risk evidence specific to supplier operations

**Does not own:** enterprise risk policy, product qualification or provider integration mechanics.

#### Finance

**Purpose:** Own deterministic commercial economics, margin, exposure calculations and financial outcome models.

**Owns:**

- unit economics model
- margin calculation
- forecast assumption set
- financial outcome record

**Does not own:** budget approval, delegated exposure policy or payment provider integration.

#### Brand

**Purpose:** Own brand identity, voice, visual system and brand-consistency rules for accepted products and ventures.

**Owns:**

- brand identity
- brand voice
- visual language
- brand guideline version
- brand consistency assessment

**Does not own:** legal policy, campaign budget or product acceptance.

#### Marketing

**Purpose:** Own campaign plans, audience strategy, creative intent, campaign lifecycle and performance interpretation.

**Owns:**

- campaign plan
- audience definition
- creative brief
- campaign lifecycle state
- campaign performance interpretation

**Does not own:** ad-network provider mechanics, enterprise budget authority or raw identity data.

#### Sales and Pricing

**Purpose:** Own offer construction, price recommendations, sales rules and commercial presentation.

**Owns:**

- offer
- price model
- discount rule
- commercial eligibility rule
- sales performance record

**Does not own:** tax law, payment settlement, product master data or marketing campaign execution.

#### Customer

**Purpose:** Own the business view of the customer relationship and permitted customer-service interactions.

**Owns:**

- customer profile projection
- customer preference
- consent projection
- customer service case
- relationship status

**Does not own:** authentication identity, payment credentials or external CRM implementation details.

#### Inventory

**Purpose:** Own inventory position, availability, reservation and stock-policy decisions.

**Owns:**

- inventory position
- stock reservation
- availability projection
- reorder decision

**Does not own:** supplier qualification, warehouse-provider integration or order lifecycle truth.

#### Orders

**Purpose:** Own the authoritative order aggregate and every valid order-state transition.

**Owns:**

- order
- order line
- order state transition
- cancellation state
- refund intent

**Does not own:** shipment truth, payment-provider internals or customer identity credentials.

#### Fulfillment

**Purpose:** Own shipment coordination, delivery progress, fulfillment exception handling and return coordination.

**Owns:**

- fulfillment case
- shipment projection
- delivery exception
- return coordination record

**Does not own:** order truth, carrier implementation details or customer-service policy.

#### Analytics

**Purpose:** Own governed outcome records, enterprise metrics and analytical projections derived from domain events.

**Owns:**

- analytical metric definition
- outcome record
- performance projection
- decision feedback signal

**Does not own:** source-domain transactional records.

### Intelligence domain group

#### Market Intelligence

**Purpose:** Convert governed external and internal evidence into market opportunities, assumptions and confidence-scored recommendations.

**Owns:**

- market evidence package
- opportunity hypothesis
- confidence assessment
- evidence freshness record

**Does not own:** product acceptance or commercial execution.

#### Specialist Intelligence

**Purpose:** Provide domain-specific analysis and recommendations without assuming ownership of the target domain.

**Owns:**

- recommendation
- explanation
- confidence and uncertainty record
- evidence reference set

**Does not own:** authoritative commerce state.

#### Executive Planning and Coordination

**Purpose:** Translate strategy and outcomes into governed cross-domain plans.

**Owns:**

- enterprise plan
- plan step
- dependency and gate model
- replan decision
- execution intent

**Does not own:** policy decisions, human-exclusive approvals or domain transactional state.

### Governance domain group

#### Policy

**Purpose:** Evaluate proposed decisions and actions against constitutional and enterprise policies.

**Owns:**

- policy definition projection
- policy evaluation request
- policy decision
- policy obligation

#### Approval

**Purpose:** Persist authenticated human decisions for constitutionally or strategically sensitive actions.

**Owns:**

- approval request
- approval evidence package
- approval decision
- replay-protection record

#### Risk

**Purpose:** Identify, classify and constrain material enterprise risk.

**Owns:**

- risk assessment
- risk classification
- mitigation requirement
- residual risk decision

#### Budget and Exposure

**Purpose:** Enforce delegated budgets, spend limits and exposure thresholds.

**Owns:**

- budget allocation
- exposure limit
- reservation
- budget decision

#### Audit

**Purpose:** Maintain immutable, attributable and correlated evidence for material decisions and actions.

**Owns:**

- audit evidence
- correlation record
- decision provenance
- tamper-evidence metadata

#### Security and Compliance

**Purpose:** Define and evaluate security posture, compliance obligations and control evidence.

**Owns:**

- security classification
- control requirement
- compliance obligation
- control evidence projection

### Platform domain group

#### Identity and Access

**Purpose:** Establish trusted human and workload identities and evaluate access rights.

**Owns:**

- identity
- credential reference
- role and permission assignment
- authentication event
- access decision

#### Durable Execution

**Purpose:** Persist and recover governed execution runs, steps, retries and compensations.

**Owns:**

- execution
- execution step
- step attempt
- idempotency record
- compensation state

#### Enterprise Events

**Purpose:** Publish, deliver, replay and observe versioned domain events.

**Owns:**

- event envelope
- outbox record
- inbox record
- delivery attempt
- dead-letter record

#### Integration

**Purpose:** Isolate external systems behind governed anti-corruption layers and provider adapters.

**Owns:**

- provider adapter configuration
- provider operation record
- normalized provider response
- provider health projection

#### Knowledge

**Purpose:** Maintain governed knowledge assets, provenance, retrieval policy and freshness.

**Owns:**

- knowledge asset
- provenance record
- retrieval index projection
- freshness status

#### Observability

**Purpose:** Correlate logs, metrics, traces and alerts across governed execution.

**Owns:**

- telemetry correlation
- service-level indicator
- alert state
- operational incident projection

#### Configuration and Deployment

**Purpose:** Own versioned runtime configuration, deployment metadata and environment promotion controls.

**Owns:**

- configuration version
- deployment release
- environment promotion decision
- rollback target

## Domain interaction rules

1. A domain may modify only data it owns.
2. Cross-domain reads use published contracts, projections or governed query services.
3. Cross-domain state changes use commands accepted by the owning domain or versioned domain events.
4. Events communicate facts that already occurred; they do not transfer ownership.
5. Synchronous calls are reserved for decisions requiring an immediate authoritative answer.
6. Long-running collaboration uses durable orchestration or choreography with explicit timeouts and compensations.
7. No domain may invoke an external provider directly; provider interaction passes through the Integration context.
8. No commerce or intelligence domain may bypass Policy, Risk, Budget, Approval, Identity or Audit controls.
9. Shared databases and cross-domain table writes are prohibited.
10. Every contract is versioned, observable and attributable.

## Context mapping patterns

| Relationship | Required pattern |
|---|---|
| Commerce or Intelligence to Governance | Customer/Supplier with Governance as authoritative upstream; synchronous decision contract where required |
| Commerce to Platform Integration | Anti-Corruption Layer plus Published Language |
| Domain to Enterprise Events | Open Host Service with versioned event envelope |
| Analytics consuming domain events | Conformist consumer of published event contracts; never owner of source truth |
| Executive Planning to operational domains | Orchestrator issuing governed intents; target domain retains decision and data ownership |
| External provider to ACOS | Anti-Corruption Layer; provider semantics never become canonical enterprise semantics |
| Human Strategic Authority to Approval | Authenticated command and durable decision evidence |

## Data ownership policy

Every canonical data object must have exactly one owning bounded context. Other contexts may maintain projections, caches or analytical copies, provided that:

- the projection is clearly marked non-authoritative;
- the source event or contract is recorded;
- freshness is measurable;
- data classification and retention rules are preserved;
- deletion or correction obligations propagate through explicit contracts.

## Domain event policy

Every domain event must include:

- globally unique event ID;
- event type and semantic version;
- occurred-at timestamp;
- producer domain and bounded context;
- aggregate or subject identifier;
- correlation and causation identifiers;
- actor or workload identity reference;
- data classification;
- payload schema reference;
- audit evidence reference where material.

Events are immutable facts. Corrections are represented by new events.

## Governance gates

A material cross-domain action may proceed only when the required gates are satisfied:

1. identity and authority resolved;
2. capability and owning domain resolved;
3. policy decision recorded;
4. risk decision recorded where applicable;
5. budget or exposure decision recorded where applicable;
6. human approval recorded where constitutionally required;
7. durable execution and idempotency established;
8. audit correlation established;
9. result validated before authoritative state is committed.

## Architectural invariants

- One business concept has one authoritative owner.
- AI recommendations never become authoritative state without the owning domain accepting them.
- Human-exclusive decisions cannot be delegated to an AI or workflow engine.
- Governance decisions fail closed.
- External-provider schemas never become canonical domain models.
- Domain contracts are explicit and versioned.
- Runtime orchestration does not own business truth.
- Analytics never writes source-domain transactional state.
- Audit evidence is append-only and correlated to every material decision and side effect.
