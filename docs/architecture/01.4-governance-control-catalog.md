# 1.4 Governance Control Catalog

## Control families

| Control family | Objective | Canonical evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Policy | Apply versioned enterprise rules consistently | decision record, policy version, evaluated facts |
| Authorization | Restrict actions to valid authority grants | subject, role, scope, limit, validity period |
| Risk | Keep residual risk within approved tolerance | inherent rating, controls, residual rating, treatment |
| Budget | Prevent unapproved financial commitments | reservation, commitment, release, reconciliation |
| Approval | Obtain accountable human or service decisions | approver identity, role, quorum, reason, timestamp |
| Segregation | Prevent conflicting duties and self-approval | requestor/approver comparison and conflict result |
| Exception | Time-box deviations with compensating controls | owner, rationale, expiry, review date |
| Audit | Preserve decision and execution evidence | immutable correlation and evidence chain |

## Standard decision effects

- `allow`: the action may proceed while the decision token remains valid.
- `deny`: the action must not execute.
- `require_approval`: execution is suspended until approval requirements are satisfied.
- `require_control`: specified controls must be demonstrated before reevaluation.
- `limit`: execution is permitted only within an explicit boundary.

Explicit deny has precedence over all other effects.

## Risk tiers

| Tier | Meaning | Minimum treatment |
|---|---|---|
| low | Limited, reversible impact | automated policy decision and audit |
| moderate | Material but bounded impact | accountable owner and standard controls |
| high | Significant financial, legal, security, privacy or brand impact | independent approval and enhanced evidence |
| critical | Enterprise-threatening or irreversible impact | executive authority, multi-party approval and recovery plan |

## Approval patterns

- Single accountable approver for moderate, reversible actions.
- Sequential domain and governance approval for cross-domain material actions.
- Quorum approval for critical actions.
- Independent security, privacy or legal approval when the relevant risk is high.
- Finance approval before commitments above delegated limits.
- Manual intervention when automated evidence is incomplete.

## Exception lifecycle

`requested -> assessed -> approved | rejected -> active -> expired | revoked -> closed`

An exception must never silently renew. Renewal is a new assessment and approval decision.

## Required controls for irreversible actions

Irreversible actions require verified identity, current authority, independent approval, explicit risk acceptance, confirmed budget where applicable, execution deadline, observable outcome, reconciliation and immutable audit evidence.
