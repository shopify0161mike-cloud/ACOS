# 1.2 Data Ownership Matrix

Status: Canonical

Each authoritative business record has exactly one owning bounded context. Other domains may hold references, read models or time-bounded caches, but cannot become authoritative by replication.

## Ownership rules

- Only the owning domain validates and mutates canonical state.
- Read models identify source domain, source version and freshness.
- Cross-domain joins belong in analytical or projection layers, not transactional ownership.
- Data transfers preserve classification, provenance, retention and purpose restrictions.
- Runtime, Events and Integration store coordination records, not authoritative commerce state.
- Audit evidence is append-only and does not replace source-domain records.

## Replication constraints

Derived copies must declare:

- authoritative source;
- synchronization mechanism;
- maximum acceptable staleness;
- rebuild procedure;
- retention and deletion behavior;
- classification and access policy.

A derived store must be disposable and rebuildable from authoritative records or governed events.

## Ownership disputes

Conflicting ownership claims require an architecture decision record. Until resolved, writes fail closed and no shared database table may be introduced as a shortcut.
